Project Abstract

Independent pedestrian mobility poses severe sensory challenges for visually impaired individuals, as traditional white canes only detect immediate physical contact, while constant auditory voice feedback causes cognitive overload in noisy urban settings. ClearStep introduces an edge-optimized, multimodal assistive navigation framework that unifies real-time semantic segmentation, 3-zone spatial path clearance analysis, on-demand vision-language reasoning, and directional tactile haptics. By integrating a lightweight SeaFormer-B architecture accelerated via NVIDIA TensorRT FP16, the system isolates 19 Cityscapes hazard classes with 72.4% mIoU in just 14.2ms. Spatial clearances across Left, Center, and Right zones are translated into non-intrusive haptic vibrations (50ms confirmation vs. [100,100,100]ms collision alert) and selective speech feedback, achieving a closed-loop response of 16.2ms to ensure swift obstacle avoidance while keeping the user's hearing open to ambient traffic.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE MODULES

AIML Methodology & Components

Three decoupled edge components designed for sub-20ms real-time execution: perception, reasoning, and tactile feedback.

Module 01 / Computer Vision

SeaFormer-B Segmentation

  • Three-Zone Path Tracking: Real-time clearance analysis across Left, Center, and Right paths.
  • 19 Hazard Categories: Isolates curbs, stairs, poles, moving vehicles, and pedestrians.
  • Sub-20ms Latency: 60 FPS TensorRT FP16 edge inference for instant reflexes.
  • 70% Contrast Masks: High-visibility overlays designed for low-vision clarity.
Module 02 / Multimodal VLM

Scene Understanding & QA

  • Situational Voice Q&A: Answers questions like "What is blocking my path?" in natural English.
  • Hands-Free Speech Input: Voice activation via microphone or R shortcut.
  • Server-Side Audio: Google TTS (gTTS) base64 MP3 stream with zero browser speech dependencies.
  • Spatial Proximity: Spoken relative distance and directional orientation.
Module 03 / Actuation

Directional Spatial Haptics

  • Directional Course Taps: Gentle 50ms vibration confirming clear straight path.
  • Collision Alert: Urgent triple-pulse ([100, 100, 100]ms) to halt immediately.
  • Emergency SOS Sequence: High-intensity distress pattern for unexpected hazards.
  • Auditory Protection: Keeps ears free to hear traffic and ambient surroundings.
